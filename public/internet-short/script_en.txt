What if tomorrow morning you wake up?
You grab your phone, and there's no internet?
No internet at all, no Wi-Fi, no mobile data.
Imagine you can't check the news, send a message,
watch a video.
The first day without internet is panic.
People go outside because they can't stay home
without content.
Children play outside because tablets don't work.
The second day is silence, nobody knows what's
happening in the world.
ATMs don't work, cards aren't accepted, money can't
be transferred.
The third day people start talking in person.
They meet in cafes, parks, streets.
The fourth day new habits appear.
People read books, walk, exercise.
The fifth day doesn't change.
Maybe we're too dependent on the internet.
Or the internet is just a tool, not the meaning of life.
