Tax Loss Harvesting in 60 Seconds.
What is it?
You sell investments that lost value.
This creates a realized loss.
That loss offsets your capital gains.
Lower gains mean lower taxes.
You save money.
But you stay invested.
Buy a similar asset.
Not identical.
IRS wash sale rule.
30 days before or after.
No identical security.
Simple example.
Stock drops 10000 dollars.
Sell it. Loss realized.
Buy similar ETF.
Offset 10000 gain.
Tax saved.
Repeat yearly.
Harvest losses.
Reduce taxes.
Keep growing.
That is Tax Loss Harvesting.
