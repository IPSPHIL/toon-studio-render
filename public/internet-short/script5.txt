What if humans could fly.
Not with machines.
With our own wings.
Imagine waking up
and just... floating.
No gravity pulling down.
We would soar above cities.
Above mountains.
Above clouds.
The world would look
so small from up there.
We would see borders disappear.
We would see how connected
everything really is.
Maybe we would finally
understand.
We are all one.
One sky.
One earth.
One family.
Flying together.
