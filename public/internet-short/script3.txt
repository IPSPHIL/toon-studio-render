What if you woke up tomorrow
and animals could talk.
Not just mimic sounds.
Real words.
Real thoughts.
Your dog looks at you
and says good morning.
Your cat judges your outfit.
A pigeon on the street
asks for directions.
Chaos.
Absolute chaos.
Animals have opinions.
Cows refuse to be milked.
Pigs demand better food.
Birds file noise complaints.
Humans are no longer alone
at the top.
We would have to negotiate.
Share the planet.
Listen.
Really listen.
Maybe we would learn
to be kinder.
To all living things.
