What if tomorrow morning, all technology stopped working?
No phones, no computers, no electricity.
The world goes quiet.
People look up from their screens for the first time in years.
Children play outside.
Neighbors talk to each other.
The first day is chaos.
Traffic lights are dead.
Shops close.
Banks cannot process payments.
The second day is silence.
No notifications. No messages. No news.
People start reading books again.
They walk to work.
They cook real food.
The third day, something changes.
People remember what it feels like to be present.
They look at the sky.
They listen to birds.
The fourth day, new habits form.
People exercise. They garden. They create.
The fifth day, they realize something.
Maybe we were too dependent on technology.
Maybe technology was just a tool, not the meaning of life.
