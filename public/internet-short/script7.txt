What if time stopped.
Everything freezes.
A bird mid-flight.
A drop of rain.
Suspended in air.
People frozen mid-step.
Mid-laugh.
Mid-tear.
The world becomes a painting.
A photograph of a moment.
You could walk through it.
Touch the frozen rain.
Look at faces.
Frozen in joy.
Frozen in pain.
You would see
how beautiful
a single moment is.
How precious.
How fragile.
Time is not our enemy.
Time is our canvas.
Every second is a stroke.
Every breath is a color.
Time stopped.
And you finally see.
Life is art.
