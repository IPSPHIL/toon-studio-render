What if gravity disappeared
for just one day.
Everything floats.
Water drifts upward.
Cars lift off the ground.
People bump into ceilings.
Chaos in the streets.
Chaos in the sky.
But then something beautiful.
Everyone is equal.
No one can fall.
No one can be pushed down.
We float together.
Maybe we would finally
see each other.
Really see each other.
Floating side by side.
No hierarchy.
No up or down.
Just us.
Together.
