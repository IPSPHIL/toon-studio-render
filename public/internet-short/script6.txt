What if you could read minds.
Every thought.
Every secret.
Every lie.
You would hear
what people really think.
About you.
About themselves.
About the world.
It would be overwhelming.
So many voices.
So many fears.
So many dreams.
But then you would learn.
Everyone is scared.
Everyone is lonely.
Everyone wants to be loved.
Maybe that is the secret.
We are all the same.
Deep down.
We just want connection.
We just want understanding.
Read minds.
And choose kindness.
